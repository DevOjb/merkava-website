# Merkava — Client Content and Business Confirmation Checklist

The frontend includes placeholders and sample values. The client/business owner should confirm the following before the backend and production launch.

## Business identity
- [ ] Correct registered/business display name and logo.
- [ ] Approved tagline and brand colours.
- [ ] Official telephone and WhatsApp numbers.
- [ ] Public email address and physical address.
- [ ] Official social media profile URLs.
- [ ] Domain name and final canonical website URL.

## Routes and schedules
- [ ] Confirm scheduled routes (currently Jos ⇄ Abuja).
- [ ] Confirm whether any additional routes are scheduled or charter-only.
- [ ] Confirm terminal/pickup/drop-off addresses in both cities.
- [ ] Confirm departure days and times.
- [ ] Confirm expected journey duration and boarding time.
- [ ] Confirm fares, currency, taxes/fees and fare-change rules.
- [ ] Confirm timezone used for all departure schedules.

## Fleet and seat inventory
- [ ] Confirm each vehicle's public name/type.
- [ ] Confirm registration/plate number where it should be displayed.
- [ ] Confirm passenger capacity (excluding driver, if applicable).
- [ ] Confirm seat numbering and vehicle seat maps.
- [ ] Confirm active/maintenance vehicle status.
- [ ] Supply approved, correctly licensed vehicle photos.

## Booking and payment policies
- [ ] Confirm passenger information required.
- [ ] Confirm whether all passengers need individual phone numbers.
- [ ] Confirm booking amendment rules.
- [ ] Confirm cancellation cut-off, charges and refund policy.
- [ ] Confirm whether a no-refund policy is legally and commercially approved.
- [ ] Select payment provider and supported payment methods.
- [ ] Confirm receipt/ticket content and customer notification channels.

## Charter
- [ ] Confirm vehicle categories and passenger options.
- [ ] Confirm charter quote and acceptance workflow.
- [ ] Confirm staff contact/response expectations.
- [ ] Confirm required information for long-distance/international charter enquiries.

## Customer content and legal
- [ ] Replace sample testimonials with approved real customer feedback.
- [ ] Confirm About and service descriptions.
- [ ] Confirm Terms & Conditions.
- [ ] Prepare privacy notice and cookie/analytics disclosures if applicable.
- [ ] Confirm feedback publication/moderation consent.
- [ ] Confirm referral programme rules, if retained.

## Operations
- [ ] Identify authorised admin/staff accounts.
- [ ] Define booking support and escalation contacts.
- [ ] Define who can edit routes, fares, schedules, fleet and cancellations.
- [ ] Define refund/payment reconciliation owner.
- [ ] Confirm data retention and customer support procedures.
